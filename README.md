# Call-Center-Analysis-PBI-Tool

## Introduction
The call center plays a critical role in delivering timely and effective customer support, serving as the primary point of contact between customers and the organization. Efficient call handling, rapid response times, effective issue resolution, and high customer satisfaction are essential indicators of service quality and operational effectiveness.

An analysis of the call center performance dashboard, which covers call response, resolution rates, agent productivity, call volumes, and customer satisfaction, reveals that while the center demonstrates strong operational efficiency—particularly in call answering speed—there are notable gaps in service quality and customer experience. These findings underscore the importance of targeted performance enhancements to ensure that operational success translates into enhanced customer satisfaction and sustained service excellence.

## Problem Statement
The disconnect between operational efficiency and customer satisfaction suggests underlying challenges related to issue resolution quality, workload distribution among agents, and customer engagement during interactions. If left unaddressed, these issues may lead to increased customer dissatisfaction, repeat calls, and reputational risk to the organization.

## Objectives
1. To improve call answer rates by reducing the proportion of unanswered calls from 18.92% through better staffing and call distribution strategies.

2. To increase issue resolution effectiveness by raising the call resolution rate, with a focus on first-call resolution.

3. To enhance customer satisfaction levels by improving interaction quality, communication effectiveness, and problem ownership during calls.

4. To optimize agent performance and workload balance by leveraging best practices from high-performing agents and preventing agent overload during peak periods.

5. To strengthen service quality monitoring and feedback mechanisms by using performance data and customer feedback to drive continuous improvement.
